My career began as a factory production operator — standing on the line at 6 AM, hitting strict output targets, and seeing firsthand how tiny friction points on the floor quietly compound into massive bottlenecks.

From the production floor, I moved into warehouse staff operations — unloading shipments, counting stock by hand, and preparing daily dispatches. I learned that a single inventory miscount never stays inside the warehouse: it cascades into delayed orders, frustrated customers, and hours of late-night reconciliation.

When I stepped up as Warehouse & Purchase Manager at Kembang Cikarang, overseeing supplier procurement and high-volume Shopee and TikTok Shop fulfillment, one truth became undeniable: most operational chaos isn't caused by lazy people. It's caused by broken tools, scattered spreadsheets, and software built by people who have never worked a shift in an actual warehouse.

"Everything I build is shaped by real operational pain I've lived myself — counting stock late at night, chasing suppliers, and fixing broken workflows."

So I started building the remedies myself. First came integrated Google Sheets engines that turned three days of monthly P&L reconciliation into a 30-second check. Then, partnering closely with my AI co-pilot Arbour, I taught myself TypeScript, PostgreSQL, relational data modeling, and modern cloud architecture — graduating from spreadsheets to shipping full-scale custom websites, WMS, ERP, and attendance systems used by real businesses every day.
